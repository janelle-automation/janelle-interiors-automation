04

Capture · behind the scenes

The reading pass

Gmail and Drive are read on the studio’s own schedule, every 10 minutes by default, and turned into records.

It stops between items, never halfway through one, and the next pass picks up where it left off. Turn Claude’s reading off and mail is still filed, with no tasks and no spend.

1

Projects from Drive

The numbered folders under CLIENTS/Projects become the job list first, so mail can be filed against them.

2

Classify each email

A quote, an order confirmation, a client approval, or machine mail from Slack or GitHub, skipped at no cost.

3

File it to the job

By the studio’s own names: “the Lemon job” and “Lemons 81326” are both Lemon Residence.

4

Raise the work

A task with one owner, a date and a next step, and a reply drafted where one is owed.

5

Read the paperwork

PDF quotes and confirmations become vendors, purchase orders and line items.

Most specific first
