Your studio's new assistant

It reads your email and Drive, keeps every project organized, and drafts the chasing — so less slips through the cracks, and you spend less time on admin.

Here's the simple version: the assistant works quietly in the background on top of the tools you already use. You keep working in Houzz Pro and Gmail. It reads what comes in, files it against the right project, prepares the replies and reminders, and shows you everything in one calm place. Nothing is ever sent without you.

01

What it does for you

Live

Reads and sorts your inbox

Every project email is read and understood — it tells a vendor quote from an order confirmation from a client approval, and files each against the right project and vendor.

Live

Turns quotes into tracked records

When a quote or confirmation arrives as a PDF, it pulls out the vendor, the items and the total, and creates the purchase order, vendor and project for you — no re-typing.

Live

Writes your replies

For each quote, confirmation or approval, it drafts a warm, professional reply — addressed to the right person, with the right people copied. You read it, tweak it, and send.

Live

Chases what's gone quiet

It watches for vendors who've gone silent, client approvals that are overdue, and delivery dates that are slipping — and prepares a gentle nudge for each, ready for you to send.

Live

Keeps each project's status current

As email comes in, a project moves itself along — spec, approval, production, shipping — so your board always reflects where things really are, without you updating it.

Live

A timeline for every project

Open any project to see everything that's happened, in order — the emails, the documents, the orders — so you can catch up on a project in seconds.

Live

Your Monday-morning summary

A weekly report of the whole studio in one read: what moved, what's on order, what's overdue, and what needs a decision from you.

Live

Every draft in one place

All the replies and reminders it writes wait for you inside the app — review, edit and send on your terms. Nothing is auto-sent, and nothing clutters your Gmail.

The three promises this system keeps

  • Nothing is ever sent without you. Every message is a draft you approve.
  • Your tools don't change. Keep working in Houzz Pro and Gmail exactly as you do today.
  • The access is yours. It's connected to your Google account and you can revoke that at any moment.
