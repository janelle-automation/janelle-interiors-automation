2. How to read this
Dual-seat is allowed. Dual-ownership of the same outcome is not. If two names could own it, the table is wrong.
Scorecards are the weekly conversation. A red number starts a problem-solve, not a blame session. Targets stay blank until one baseline week is tracked — then lock them.
1099 model: ~20 hours per seat. 20 hours means that seat, not 20 plus nights of Owner cleanup.
