Use this prompt for this specific inspiration direction:
PRIMARY BATHROOM MOODBOARD PROMPT
Act as my Senior Interior Designer and Luxury Residential Design Director.
I am providing several bathroom inspiration images. Study them carefully and create a cohesive primary bathroom moodboard that closely matches the visual language, material palette, warmth, and architectural character of these references.
Do not create a generic luxury bathroom. The design should clearly reflect the specific style shown in the inspiration images.
OVERALL DESIGN STYLE
The bathroom should feel:
* Warm
 * European
 * Quiet luxury
 * Organic
 * Refined
 * Textural
 * Architectural
 * Sophisticated
 * Slightly Mediterranean
 * Contemporary with traditional detailing
 * Custom rather than trendy
The palette should remain soft and tonal, with contrast coming primarily from natural stone veining, warm wood, aged metal, and subtle architectural details.
Avoid bright white, cool gray, stark black-and-white contrast, overly modern cabinetry, or anything that feels commercial.
PRIMARY COLOR PALETTE
Use a restrained palette of:
* Warm ivory
 * Cream
 * Putty
 * Sand
 * Soft taupe
 * Pale natural oak
 * Warm marble white
 * Muted plum or burgundy veining
 * Aged brass / brushed bronze
The overall feeling should be sun-washed and soft.
⸻
KEY DESIGN ELEMENTS
1. VANITY + MILLWORK
Use light natural oak cabinetry as a major design element.
Match the inspiration with:
* Rift-cut or straight-grain oak
 * Soft natural matte finish
 * Warm blonde tone
 * Thin furniture-style reveals
 * Inset or refined framed cabinetry
 * Large drawers
 * Minimal ornamentation
Incorporate select fluted or reeded drawer fronts as an elevated detail.
Cabinetry should feel custom, European, and handcrafted.
Avoid orange oak, rustic grain, shaker-heavy cabinetry, or dark stains.
⸻
2. VANITY STONE
Use a dramatic but sophisticated natural stone with:
* Cream or warm white background
 * Gray veining
 * Plum, burgundy, mauve, or charcoal movement
 * Strong organic patterning
Consider a stone similar in feeling to:
* Calacatta Viola
 * Arabescato Corchia
 * Breccia Capraia
 * Paonazzo
 * Other warm dramatic Italian marbles
Use the stone for:
* Countertop
 * Integrated or undermount sink area
 * Backsplash
 * Shelf ledges
 * Select niche details
The stone should become a signature decorative element.
⸻
3. CHECKERED MARBLE FLOOR
Use a checkerboard natural stone floor, but keep it refined and muted.
Match the inspiration using a blend of:
* Warm beige or limestone
 * White marble
 * Gray-veined marble
 * Occasional plum or dark veining
The checkerboard should feel collected and European, not retro.
Use a honed finish rather than high gloss.
The pattern should feel natural, slightly varied, and handcrafted.
⸻
4. SHOWER WALLS
Use a seamless warm plaster or plaster-look waterproof finish.
Target the appearance of:
* Tadelakt
 * Lime plaster
 * Microcement
 * Mineral plaster
Color:
* Soft ivory
 * Warm beige
 * Pale putty
 * Creamy stone
Keep the shower walls almost monolithic.
The shower should feel calm and architectural rather than tile-heavy.
⸻
5. SHOWER NICHE
Create a strong architectural niche inspired by the references.
Use:
* Arched or softened niche shape
 * Marble ledge
 * Decorative tile or vertical tile inside the niche
 * Warm natural stone detailing
 * Refined trim
Consider using a vertical handmade tile or slim-format tile inside the niche to create texture against the plaster walls.
The niche should feel intentional and custom.
⸻
6. SHOWER FLOOR
Use a quiet, slip-resistant shower floor that does not compete with the main bathroom floor.
Consider:
* Warm honed limestone
 * Small-format marble mosaic
 * Cream stone mosaic
 * Pale microcement-look surface
Keep the shower floor tonal with the plaster walls.
⸻
7. FREESTANDING TUB
Include a sculptural freestanding tub with:
* Soft oval shape
 * Matte white finish
 * Minimal profile
 * Rounded edges
 * Clean, simple silhouette
The bathtub should be surrounded by warm architecture rather than standing in an overly decorative setting.
⸻
8. PLUMBING FINISH
Use a warm metal finish throughout.
Preferred direction:
* Brushed brass
 * Aged brass
 * Satin bronze
 * Soft champagne bronze
Avoid very yellow polished brass.
Use wall-mounted faucets wherever appropriate to reinforce the European architectural feeling.
⸻
9. HARDWARE
Use elegant, understated hardware.
Consider:
* Slim brass T-bars
 * Simple cylindrical pulls
 * Small round knobs
 * Select decorative hardware with subtle stone or jewel-like inserts
Keep hardware refined and slightly jewelry-like without becoming ornate.
⸻
10. LIGHTING
Select sculptural decorative sconces with:
* Warm brass backplates
 * Soft white glass
 * Alabaster
 * Ribbed or sculptural glass
 * Organic forms
Lighting should feel artisanal and European.
Avoid generic vanity bars or overly contemporary black fixtures.
⸻
11. MIRRORS
Use mirrors with:
* Softly rounded corners
 * Subtle irregular or scalloped forms
 * Thin aged-brass or bronze frames
 * Vintage European influence
Avoid sharp modern rectangles unless softened by surrounding architecture.
⸻
MOODBOARD LAYOUT
Create a polished luxury interior design presentation board.
Use a warm ivory background with generous negative space.
Include:
HERO IMAGE
One large inspiration image establishing the overall bathroom character.
MATERIAL PALETTE
Show individual material samples for:
* Natural oak
 * Dramatic marble
 * Checkerboard marble
 * Warm plaster
 * Shower niche tile
 * Shower floor material
 * Brass finish
FEATURE ELEMENTS
Include isolated visual references for:
* Freestanding tub
 * Vanity design
 * Wall-mounted faucet
 * Decorative sconce
 * Mirror
 * Cabinet hardware
DETAIL IMAGES
Include several small detail moments showing:
* Reeded cabinetry
 * Marble edge detail
 * Arched niche
 * Plaster texture
 * Brass plumbing
 * Natural stone veining
⸻
DESIGN PRIORITY
The finished moodboard should communicate:
warm European architecture + natural oak cabinetry + expressive Italian marble + plaster shower walls + sculptural forms + restrained aged brass detailing.
It should feel like a bathroom designed for a high-end California residence influenced by European boutique hotels and Mediterranean interiors.
The design should feel layered and luxurious, but never flashy.
MOODBOARD TITLE
PRIMARY BATHROOM — DESIGN INSPIRATION
Subtitle:
Natural Oak • Honed Marble • Warm Plaster • Aged Brass
Based on these images, I would make the dramatic Viola-style marble + pale oak + warm plaster the three anchor materials. The checker floor can then connect all three without making the room feel busy.
